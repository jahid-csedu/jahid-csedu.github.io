Personal product · 2025 —

NewzGator

The product I keep building. A newsroom for Bangladeshi papers: one story, once, in the right section, with a summary, even when many sites publish it.

Ingest

Configured crawlers find new links. A transactional outbox keeps them if the broker is down. The scraper retries, then fans the article out.

Understand

Section from the URL when it is obvious, otherwise an ONNX model for Bengali. Summaries from Groq, with Gemini behind them. Headlines stored as vectors in PostgreSQL.

Read

The feed shows the lead article of each cluster. Search, related stories, and recommendations ranked from clicks, bookmarks, comments, and reactions.

Operate

An admin console for publisher configs and pipeline health. Gatling against the reader API. Traces through OpenTelemetry.
